    One round from take-off to vanish

    Every round follows a fixed pattern. Bets open for a short window, the plane starts climbing, and the multiplier rises from 1.00x. At a point set before the round begins, the plane disappears and the round ends. Any player who has cashed out before that moment keeps their stake multiplied by the current figure; anyone still in loses the stake.

    Because the result is locked in advance, later bets do not change it. Past rounds also do not shape the next one, which is why bonuskong official guidance always stresses independent outcomes and careful stake control rather than chasing a pattern.
